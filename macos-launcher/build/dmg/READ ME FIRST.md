# Maths Adventure Island — Parent Guide

A maths learning game for young children (built for age 5 / UKG). It runs entirely on your Mac: no internet, no accounts, no ads, no purchases, and no data leaves the computer.

## Install

1. Open **Maths-Adventure.dmg** (or unzip **Maths-Adventure-macOS.zip**).
2. Drag **Maths Adventure** into **Applications**.
3. Double-click **Maths Adventure**. It opens in its own Google Chrome window.

If macOS says the app "can't be opened because it is from an unidentified developer" (this happens when the file was downloaded or copied from another Mac), **right-click → Open → Open** once. After that, a normal double-click works.

Google Chrome must be installed. If it isn't, the launcher offers to open the app in Safari instead.

## First time

Fill in the setup screen with your child:

- **Name or nickname** (optional)
- **Age** and **school class** (these are separate settings)
- **Difficulty**: Gentle, Just right, or Challenge
- **Ollie's voice**:
  - **Hindi** (default): Ollie speaks proper Hindi, numbers included (एक, दो, तीन), using the Mac's Hindi voice *Lekha*. The screen shows simple Hinglish.
  - **Hinglish**: an Indian-English voice.
  - **English**.
  - **Voice speed** (Very slow, Slow, Normal; Slow is the default) is in Grown-ups → Settings.
- **Sound**: on or off

Age 5 / UKG is fully supported. Other ages currently use the same Age-5 curriculum, and the app says so honestly.

## What your child can do

- **Maths Adventure Island**: 9 zones (Counting Forest, Number Mountain, Addition Castle, Subtraction Cave, Compare Lake, Shape City, Pattern Garden, Puzzle Beach, Time Meadow). Every zone is always open. Each zone offers:
  - **Learn**: a short, hands-on lesson
  - **Play a mission**: a mix of different games
  - Any single game the child chooses
- **Today's Journey**: a guided 10–15 minute session. It goes: warm-up, a short lesson, a few games, a slightly harder challenge, then a celebration.
- **Treasure Hunt**: 5 map stops, each a different game, ending with a treasure chest.
- **Free Play**: a mix of everything.
- **Quiz**: set up by you (see below).
- **Sticker book**: one sticker per finished session, earned in a fixed order (no random prizes). Also badges, and island friends who appear as stickers are collected.

The 13 game types are: Count the Animals, Feed the Monster, Balloon Pop, Number Train, Treasure Chest, Pizza Maths, Shape Detective, Catch the Number, Pattern Puzzle, Toy Shop, Frog Jumps (number line), Block Towers, and Look & Choose.

Every drag-and-drop can also be done by **tapping** or clicking.

There are **no timers**. A wrong answer gets a gentle hint and another try. After 3 tries, Ollie shows the answer and explains it. Your child can stop at any time with the **✋ Stop** button.

## Grown-ups area (🔒 button)

To open it, answer a quick multiplication question. This keeps little fingers out; it is **not** a password or a security feature.

| Tab | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Questions answered; the **right-first-time** rate (retries don't count towards it); eventually-right rate; days played; a weekly trend; suggested next topics |
| **Topics** | Level per topic, with − / + to change it; status; recent answers; *why* the level last changed; the automatic-difficulty switch |
| **Quizzes** | Set up a quiz: choose topics (or mixed), level, 5/10/15/20 questions. Then review every answer from finished quizzes |
| **Mistakes** | Where your child needed more tries, with examples |
| **History** | Every play session |
| **Settings** | Name, age, class, language, difficulty style, automatic difficulty, **advanced challenges** (levels 4–5), session length, sound, voice, reduced motion, switch or add children |
| **Data** | Download a backup (JSON), export answers (CSV), restore a backup, reset progress, delete a profile |

### How difficulty changes

The app only looks at answers at the current level in each topic.

- **Moves up** after at least 6 answers with at least 80% right first time.
- **Moves down** after 5 answers with 40% or fewer right first time.
- Quizzes and lessons never change the level.
- Every change, and the reason for it, is shown in the **Topics** tab.
- Levels 1–3 cover the age-5 curriculum. Levels 4–5 (adding to 20, numbers to 50, skip-counting) stay off until you switch on **Advanced challenges**.

## Where data is stored

Progress is saved in the app's own Chrome profile on this Mac:
`~/Library/Application Support/MathsAdventure/`

It is kept if you clear your normal Chrome history. Use **Data → Download backup** now and then.
